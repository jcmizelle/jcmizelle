### J.C. (Chris) Mizelle, PhD

Neuroscientist with 20+ years designing experiments and analyzing raw neural and behavioral data: EEG/ERP (time-frequency, connectivity, graph theory), MRI, fMRI and DTI, and quantitative motor behavior. 30 peer-reviewed papers on sensorimotor integration, brain injury, and aging. Long-time journal reviewer and NSF panelist, so much of my work is judging whether an analysis actually supports its conclusions.

**Tools:** MATLAB and EEGLAB (wrote a lab toolbox for preprocessing, time-frequency, connectivity, graph theory, and permutation statistics), FSL and FreeSurfer (automated Linux/Bash pipelines for structural MRI, fMRI, and DTI), Python (NumPy, pandas, SciPy), currently moving EEG work to MNE-Python.

**Here:**
- [SMILe](https://github.com/jcmizelle/SMILe) - MATLAB EEG toolbox: preprocessing pipeline, time-frequency and cross-spectral analysis, functional connectivity, surrogate data, permutation statistics, minimum spanning trees, and network visualization
- [fMRI-Pipeline](https://github.com/jcmizelle/fMRI-Pipeline) - Bash/FSL/FreeSurfer and SPM batch pipelines for task fMRI (fieldmap correction, FEAT, conjunctions, PPI) and TRACULA diffusion tractography
